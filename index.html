ABOUTThe Author

关于我About

我是 Norris Zhu，做数据科学和人工智能，平时也爱写点代码、记录想法。

生活里离不开抹茶，奶茶只点抹茶，咖啡也要换燕麦奶；闲下来就去打篮球，水平一般，但上场就图个痛快。

最近对 AI Agent 和自动驾驶很上头，觉得这两块是接下来最有意思的方向。

做这行说到底是对"智能"本身好奇，也想借着技术去看看世界，找到一些真正能打动自己的东西。

I'm Norris Zhu. I work in data science and artificial intelligence, and I write to think.

Off the clock it's matcha — matcha milk tea, oat-milk lattes — and pickup basketball where I'm average at best but play for the fun of it.

Right now I'm hooked on AI agents and autonomous driving, the two directions I find most interesting.

At bottom it's curiosity about intelligence itself — and a hope that the work takes me out to see the world.
